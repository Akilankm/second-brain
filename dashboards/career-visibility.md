# Career Visibility Dashboard

## Visibility equation

**Visibility = Useful artifacts × business relevance × repeatability × stakeholder trust**

Raw activity is not visibility. The strongest signal is when another person can reuse your work to make a faster or safer decision.

## Artifact ladder

### Level 1 — Understand
- concise domain notes
- regulatory summaries
- architecture comparisons

### Level 2 — Translate
- business problem → AI pattern
- regulation → engineering control
- client pain → measurable outcome

### Level 3 — Build
- working demos
- evaluation harnesses
- reference architectures
- reusable components

### Level 4 — Enable
- templates adopted by others
- design-review checklists
- reusable accelerators
- internal learning sessions

### Level 5 — Influence
- shape solution approach
- identify reusable IP
- contribute to proposals/PoCs
- help teams avoid risk or rework

## High-signal behaviors for a C2 AI role

- Speak in business process language before model language.
- Bring an architecture diagram when a discussion is ambiguous.
- Add evaluation and controls before someone asks for them.
- Quantify baseline, target and business value.
- Separate demo-grade from production-grade clearly.
- Capture decisions and assumptions in concise notes.
- Reuse before rebuilding.
- Make other engineers faster.

## Avoid

- chasing visibility through message volume
- generic AI trend forwarding
- demos without controls or metrics
- claiming production readiness without evidence
- bypassing internal governance or client confidentiality
- publishing client/internal information into this public repository

> **Important:** this repository is public. Keep all client names, proprietary TCS material, credentials, internal architecture, source code and confidential project details out of it.
