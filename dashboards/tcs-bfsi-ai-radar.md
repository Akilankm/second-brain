## 2. Agentic AI orchestration in BFSI

### TCS Cognitive Automation Platform — `live-capability`
TCS publicly describes its Cognitive Automation Platform as an agentic-AI orchestration platform spanning business and IT operations. Published features include:

- an **agentic mesh** and marketplace
- **200+ pre-built reusable domain-trained agents**
- agent studio/builder
- enterprise guardrails and policy controls
- knowledge/context layers
- RAG and knowledge-fabric patterns
- governance and observability
- human approvals/overrides
- multimodal and composite-AI capabilities

TCS also states **60+ wins** and **30+ live implementations** on the product page.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/what-we-do/industries/insurance/solution/cognitive-automation-platform-transform-banking

See [[ai/agentic-ai-bfsi-architecture]] and [[tcs/tcs-bfsi-ai-offerings]].

TCS launched a Google Cloud Gemini Experience Center inside its BFSI Innovation Lab in Bengaluru on 22 August 2025. The public announcement describes agentic-AI work for regulatory compliance, customer service, decision workflows and back-office operations, and access to TCS BaNCS on Google Cloud.

On 11 August 2026, TCS said its ninth GEC contained **3,000+ industry- and context-aware AI agents** built with Gemini Enterprise. Demonstrations included fraud investigation for financial institutions and automated insurance claims.

## 5. TCS BaNCS AI layer

### TCS BaNCS AI Compass — `announced/live-capability`
TCS announced AI Compass on 19 December 2025 as an AI core for TCS BaNCS spanning ML, deep learning, GenAI and pre-built agents. Public descriptions include banking agents for onboarding, underwriting and customer queries, securities use cases, no-code lifecycle capabilities, guardrails and audit logging.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-bancs-ai-upgrade-new-core-tool-supercharge-innovation

### TCS BaNCS Corporate Actions — `deployed / product-capability`
TCS' current product page says **TCS BaNCS for Corporate Actions is deployed at more than 65 clients worldwide** and explicitly lists AI for operational support, ML for error processing/repair, chatbots and predictive analytics. At ISITC 2026, TCS separately described the offering as an **AI-enabled intelligent solution deployed at more than 60 leading financial institutions**; at SIFMA Ops 2026 it said the solution is used by **eight of the world's top ten custodians**.

Evidence boundary: this proves a widely deployed platform with published AI/ML capabilities and an explicit TCS “AI-enabled” characterization; it does **not** prove every client installation has every AI capability activated.

Sources:
- https://www.tcs.com/what-we-do/products-platforms/tcs-bancs/solution/corporate-actions
- https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-bancs-isitc-annual-securities-operations-summit-2026
- https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-bancs-sifma-ops-2026

### TCS BaNCS IX + Quartz Intelligent Insights — `announced`
On 23 January 2025 TCS announced GenAI capabilities in TCS BaNCS IX and Quartz Intelligent Insights and said it was working with customers including tier-1 insurers on **two pilots and three projects**.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/news-alert/tcs-offers-genai-based-solutions-help-financial-institutions-enhance-customer-experience-improve-reporting

## 7. TCS public architecture vocabulary

TCS public BFSI material currently emphasizes:

- **context fabric** for process, regulatory, domain and data context
- agentic orchestration / agent mesh
- enterprise knowledge layers and RAG
- composite predictive AI + GenAI
- governance, guardrails and observability
- human oversight / approval
- explainability and auditability
- AI-ready data and cloud foundations
- operational **control-plane** thinking in post-trade/corporate-actions workflows
