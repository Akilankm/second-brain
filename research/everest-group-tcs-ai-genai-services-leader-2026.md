# TCS Named a Leader in AI and Generative AI Services by Everest Group (2026)

> Public-only note. This records TCS-published disclosure of an analyst recognition; it is not independent verification of every claim in the underlying analyst report.

## Source metadata

- **Publication date:** 2026-03-09
- **Evidence labels:** `analyst-recognition`, `official-press-release`
- **Primary source:** https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/analyst-reports/tcs-named-leader-artificial-intelligence-gen-ai-services-everest-group

## Public facts

TCS states that Everest Group positioned it as a **Leader** in the PEAK Matrix for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI Services.

The TCS announcement attributes the recognition to a **platform-led AI transformation strategy** that integrates industry-specific assets and platforms including **TCS AI WisdomNext, ignio and TCS Cognix**. It also states that Everest Group cited TCS' co-innovation with hyperscalers, platform providers and niche partners to develop generative and agentic-AI solutions tailored to modernization and industry use cases.

The release further states that referenced clients highlighted TCS' price competitiveness in AI and generative-AI deals. This is retained as a TCS-reported analyst/client-evidence summary, not as an independently audited performance metric.

## BFSI relevance

The announcement is cross-industry.
