# Huntington National Bank — enterprise data, AI-first data, and context fabric signal

## Public fact

TCS Financial Services and Insurance publicly described participation in the Huntington National Bank Analytics & Data Expo 2026. The post says TCS engaged with Huntington teams on building AI-first data enterprises, advancing AI for data, leveraging enterprise context fabric, and scaling data product strategies. It names the event as a collaboration opportunity and identifies TCS-led sessions by Prabhakaran (Prab) Pitchandi and Sujata Zanjad.
