The public agenda vocabulary includes:

- composable AI;
- ownership decisions for AI capabilities;
- enterprise scaling;
- governance;
- cloud-first insurance; and
- quantum readiness.

The page also advertises sessions on AI-first insurance, connected intelligence, cloud scale, and the future of enterprise AI, plus a session titled **“The quantum horizon - Exploring the next wave of research and capabilities poised to transform the insurance industry.”**
