## Media curation rule

A link belongs here when it exposes at least one of:

- an official product/interface/demo
- an official architecture or technical vocabulary
- an official TCS BFSI leader/speaker explaining strategy
- a named/public customer implementation
- a TCS event session with public watch material
- a high-density TCS research/journal asset
- an official post containing factual details not duplicated elsewhere

Generic employer branding, hiring content and non-BFSI promotional media are excluded from this library.
