## Weekly operating loop

1. Read the radar.
2. Select one high-value BFSI signal.
3. Convert it into a one-page note or architecture.
4. Build one small reusable technical artifact where useful.
5. Capture three stakeholder questions.
6. Link the artifact back into Foam.
7. Track whether the artifact created a discussion, decision, reuse or measurable outcome.

## Core thesis

In BFSI, AI skill alone is not scarce. **Trusted AI delivery with domain context, control evidence, observability, regulatory traceability and measurable business value is scarce.** Build visibility around that intersection.
