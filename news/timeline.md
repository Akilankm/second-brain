# TCS AI + BFSI Public Timeline

[[index|← Home]] · [[dashboards/tcs-bfsi-ai-radar]] · [[sources/source-catalog]]

Chronology of high-signal public TCS AI and BFSI events. Dates below are event/announcement dates, not crawl dates.

## 2026

### 2026-10-26 — TCS BaNCS at ABA Annual Convention — `planned`
TCS BaNCS plans to discuss core modernization, AI, stablecoins, tokenization and risk management. TCS states BaNCS serves 500+ institutions worldwide and that two implementations process more than 1 billion accounts.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-bancs-at-aba-annual-convention-2026

### 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-01 — ITC Vegas — `planned`
TCS is sponsoring an AI-first insurance program with Anthropic, AWS and FICO. Published topics include composable AI, ownership decisions, enterprise scaling, governance, cloud-first insurance and quantum readiness.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-at-itc-vegas-2026

### 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-01 — TCS BaNCS and Quartz at Sibos — `planned`
TCS says BaNCS will showcase payments and securities capabilities across 500+ installations; BaNCS for Payments powers 80+ clearing systems. Quartz is being positioned around AI + DLT for digital assets, digital currencies, compliance/KYC and AI-powered surveillance.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-bancs-quartz-sibos-2026-miami

### 2026-09-24 — Risk Live North America — `planned`
TCS plans a roundtable on scaling AI and agentic intelligence across risk and compliance, including governance, monitoring, adoption metrics, auditable models, bias mitigation and human oversight.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-at-risk-live-north-america-2026

### 2026-09-09 — RBI emerging-technology prudence / AI governance speech — `regulatory / official-speech`
RBI Deputy Governor Rohit Jain described AI as increasingly augmenting human judgment in finance and framed three technology-amplified risks as **speed, concentration and opacity**. The speech says higher-consequence uses such as autonomous credit approval or transaction execution should attract stronger governance, validation, oversight and intervention expectations, and reiterates that responsibility does not disappear when models or technology are supplied by third parties.

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_SpeechesView.aspx?Id=1577

### 2026-09-08 — Odisha AI-enabled digital governance platform — `announced`
TCS announced a ₹122 crore bid win to build an AI-enabled digital governance platform for the Odisha Government. This is a TCS-wide AI signal rather than a BFSI program.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-wins-122-crore-bid-build-ai-enabled-digital-governance-platform-odisha-government

### 2026-09-07 — AI-native creative engineering studio in UK — `announced`
TCS announced an AI-native creative engineering studio in the UK. This is included as a current company-wide AI capability signal.
