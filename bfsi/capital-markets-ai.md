# TCS Capital Markets, Securities and Wealth AI — Public Intelligence

[[bfsi/domain-map|← BFSI map]] · [[tcs/tcs-bfsi-ai-offerings]] · [[news/timeline]]

## TCS BaNCS AI Compass — securities / wealth — `announced/live-capability`

TCS announced AI Compass on 19 December 2025 as an AI core for TCS BaNCS. Public securities examples include:

- tax-treatment interpretation
- dividend/interest classification
- identifying missing corporate-action data
- document interpretation

TCS says the AI core spans banking, securities and wealth management and includes guardrails, traceability, explainability and audit logging.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-bancs-ai-upgrade-new-core-tool-supercharge-innovation

## Quartz Intelligent Insights — `announced/live-capability`

On 23 January 2025 TCS announced Quartz Intelligent Insights, describing GenAI applied across databases, distributed-ledger systems, unstructured data and big-data environments for financial institutions.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/news-alert/tcs-offers-genai-based-solutions-help-financial-institutions-enhance-customer-experience-improve-reporting

## TCS BaNCS securities / payments scale — `reported by TCS`

For Sibos 2026, TCS states:

- BaNCS solutions have **500+ installations globally**
- BaNCS for Payments powers **80+ clearing systems**
- BaNCS Global Securities Platform supports post-trade operations across T+0/T+1 environments and multiple asset classes

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/events/tcs-bancs-quartz-sibos-2026-miami

These are broader platform scale figures and are not counts of AI deployments.
