## AI infrastructure — HyperVault

### 20 Nov 2025 — TPG investment — `announced`
TCS announced a strategic partnership with TPG to support HyperVault. TCS said combined commitments could reach **₹18,000 crore**, including up to **₹8,820 crore** from TPG, and described a plan for AI-ready data-center capacity above **1 GW**.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-secures-1bn-investment-from-tpg-accelerate-ai-data-center-business-hypervault

### 5 Sep 2026 — Telangana campus — `planned`
HyperVault said it had secured **264 acres** in Hyderabad for a phased AI data-center campus of **up to 1 GW**, designed for high-density GPU deployments for training, inference and advanced computing.

Source: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-hypervault-establish-large-scale-ai-data-center-campus-telangana

## AI model and platform ecosystem

TCS has publicly announced or expanded strategic AI relationships with:

- **Anthropic** — Global Premier Partnership; dedicated TCS business unit; regulated-industry go-to-market; 50,000 associates to receive Claude access.
- **Mistral AI** — first GSI partner for Mistral Forge; dedicated CoE; initial industry focus includes BFSI.
- **Google Cloud** — Gemini Enterprise, Gemini Experience Centers, agentic AI, Vertex/Gemini-based accelerators.
- **NVIDIA** — NVIDIA Business Unit; TCS AI Spectrum for BFSI; and the broader [[tcs/rapid-outcome-ai|TCS Rapid Outcome AI]] productionization platform, with banking explicitly listed among supported sectors.
- **Microsoft** — AI/cloud collaboration with industry solutions including financial services.
- **ServiceNow** — large-scale enterprise AI and autonomous workflow collaboration.
- **OpenAI** — multi-dimensional partnership announced by TCS in FY26 results, including AI transformation and infrastructure dimensions.
- **AMD** — co-development of industry AI/GenAI solutions and AI-ready infrastructure work described in FY26 results.

See [[tcs/ai-partnerships]] for source-by-source detail.

## Gemini Experience Center network

TCS and Google Cloud have expanded a global network of Gemini Experience Centers for co-creation, prototyping and industrialization of AI solutions.

Key public BFSI-relevant points:

- **Bengaluru, 22 Aug 2025:** GEC launched inside TCS' BFSI Innovation Lab, with TCS BaNCS access and agentic-AI BFSI use cases.
- **Mexico City, 11 Aug 2026:** TCS described its ninth GEC and said it contained **3,000+ industry- and context-aware AI agents**, including demonstrations for financial fraud investigation and insurance claims.
- **Kolkata, 16 Jul 2026:** TCS stated plans for **10 GECs globally by end-2026** and referenced 3,000 industry/context agents built with Gemini Enterprise.

Sources:

- https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-google-cloud-launch-gemini-experience-center-kolkata

### Rapid Outcome AI — productionization layer

**Launch date:** 17 Mar 2026  
**Evidence:** `announced / product-capability`

TCS launched Rapid Outcome AI with NVIDIA to move AI from experimentation into scalable production deployment. The current solution page describes a blueprint-led platform combining predictive analytics, GenAI, computer vision, agentic AI and physical AI with NVIDIA accelerated computing. TCS explicitly lists **banking** among supported industries and emphasizes simulation, monitoring, governance, reliability and operational integration.

This is a cross-industry platform signal; the cited sources do not establish a named banking customer deployment.

Sources:
- https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-launches-rapid-outcome-ai-platform-powered-nvidia
- https://www.tcs.com/what-we-do/services/artificial-intelligence/solution/tcs-rapid-outcome-ai
